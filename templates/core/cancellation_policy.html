Train Ticket Cancellation & Refund Policy

As per Ministry of Railways & IRCTC Standards

Train ticket cancellations on RailAway follow official Indian Railways passenger fare rules. Refunds are calculated based on the timing of cancellation relative to the scheduled departure / chart preparation.

Cancellation Window Refund Amount Deduction / Clerkage Fee Status Eligibility
> 48 Hours before departure 90% Base + 100% Taxes Flat clerkage: ₹60 (2S), ₹120 (SL), ₹180 (3AC/CC), ₹240 (1AC/2AC) Confirmed & RAC
12 to 48 Hours before departure 75% Base + 50% Taxes 25% of Base Fare or flat clerkage (whichever is higher) Confirmed
4 to 12 Hours before chart prep 50% Base Fare 50% of Base Fare Confirmed
< 4 Hours (Post Charting) No Online Refund 100% fare (TDR can be filed in special cases like train delay > 3h) All
Waitlist (WL) auto-drop after charting 100% Full Refund ₹0 deduction (Automatic 100% reversal to source account) Fully Waitlisted
💡 Key Train Guidelines:
  • Tatkal Tickets: Confirmed Tatkal tickets are non-refundable unless the train is cancelled or running >3 hours late.
  • RAC Tickets: RAC tickets cancelled up to 30 mins before scheduled departure are eligible for refund after deducting clerkage charge.
  • Train Cancelled by Railways: 100% full refund is automatically credited without any manual cancellation requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for the refund to reflect in my bank account?

UPI and RailAway Wallet refunds are processed instantly (typically 15 to 30 minutes). Debit/Credit Card and NetBanking refunds are credited within 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank's settlement cycle.

Can I cancel tickets for a single passenger in a group reservation?

Yes! Partial passenger cancellation is fully supported. You can select specific passengers to cancel while keeping the remaining passengers confirmed.

What happens if my train or flight is cancelled by the operator?

You receive a 100% full refund with zero deductions. RailAway automatically initiates the refund to your original payment method without requiring you to file a manual claim.

Is the convenience fee refundable upon cancellation?

Standard gateway convenience fees are non-refundable for user-initiated cancellations. However, if the trip is cancelled by the airline, railway, or bus operator, 100% of all charges including fees are refunded.

Have questions about an existing reservation?

Our customer care team is available 24 hours a day to assist you.
