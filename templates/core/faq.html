Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download or print my confirmed E-Ticket?

After completing your demo payment, you will immediately be redirected to your ticket page. You can also view and print all tickets at any time from your Dashboard > My Bookings.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancellations are accepted up to 4 hours prior to scheduled departure. Refunds are calculated automatically (up to 90% refund for cancellations >24 hours ahead). Cancelled seats are immediately released back to available inventory.

Is real payment information required?

No. This system features a simulated payment sandbox. You can use prefilled demo credentials or test cards without entering any real banking details.
